I'm Wendy Chairez, the human behind Hollywood Bark. Since November 2023, I've been caring for dogs right from my home in San Antonio, Texas — no kennels, no concrete runs, just couches, backyards, and a whole lot of love.

A little clarity, a lot of care

Happy pups. Clear policies.

The short version: clear rules keep every pup safe and every family happy. 💛

  • Current vaccinations required: rabies, distemper/parvo, and bordetella.
  • Flea & tick prevention required.
  • We recommend spayed/neutered dogs for group settings.
  • Crate-trained pups preferred; we use crates for calm rest time and safety.
  • A meet & greet is required before the first stay.

  • Mon–Fri: drop-offs 2:00–6:00 PM, pickups 10:00 AM–12:00 PM, all by appointment.
  • Saturday: 11:00 AM–2:00 PM.
  • Sunday is family day: by appointment, 4:00–6:00 PM, with a +$20 Sunday fee per reservation.
  • No daycare on weekends.
  • Handoffs are quick and curbside (under 3 minutes) — pups settle faster that way!

Boarding is charged per calendar night. Check-in Mon–Fri: 2:00–6:00 PM. Check-out Mon–Fri: 10:00 AM–12:00 PM. The 12:00–2:00 PM block is protected for kennel cleaning. All pickups and drop-offs are by appointment. Late checkout after 12:00 PM: +$25 per reservation. No pickups after 6:00 PM.

We're open on holidays with holiday rates. For holiday weekends: drop-offs start Friday — no pickups or drop-offs on Saturday or Sunday. On the Monday holiday, pickups and drop-offs are available 4:00–6:00 PM only. Holiday payments are non-refundable for cancellations within 72 hours of drop-off. Closed: Christmas Day and New Year's Day.

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before drop-off. Cancellations within 48 hours are charged 50% of the reservation.

We’re a private home — clients don’t enter the house; we meet you at the door or your car. Small groups by design mean spots are limited, so holiday dates book out fast.

Questions? Text us anytime — we’re happy to help.
